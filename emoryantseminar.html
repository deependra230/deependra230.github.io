September 29 Jidong Wang Georgia Tech
Grassmannians Rediscovered

Abstract. Depending on your mathematical clan, a Grassmannian may be either a smooth manifold, an algebraic variety, or a representable functor. What do Grassmannians mean for a matroid theorist? In this talk, we will consider Grassmannians over unusual algebraic structures. They unify many phenomena in matroid theory and tropical geometry. Even basic notion such as Grassman- nian duality suggests interesting questions that led to pleasant discoveries
