I am currently a Visiting Assistant Professor at Emory University. My research interests are broadly in algebra and number theory. Here is my CV.

I organize the Emory Algebra and Number Theory Seminar.

I completed my PhD in 2024 at the University of Pennsylvania under the supervision of Prof. David Harbater. Prior to that, I worked in machine learning, including a summer with Prof. Yoseph Barash at the University of Pennsylvania, and three years at Groupon before that. I finished my undergradute studies in computer science from IIT Delhi, India in 2014.
